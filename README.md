# SWET

**A chronicle of Knox County, Kentucky — 14–31 August 2026.**

> *These are the end times. There was no hope of survival. This is how you died.*

That is what Project Zomboid tells you before you play a second of it. Over
eighteen days on a private server, three people supplied the details: thirteen
characters lost, a forge built from nothing, one man blown up by his own pipe
bomb, and the same category of roof fatal twice.

Fifteen chapters and a ledger, assembled from nineteen days of dedicated-server
logs and the group's own chat. Every quoted line in the text is verbatim.

The reader filters by survivor and by thread — deaths, roofs, bombs, building,
the journal, driving — and pages through whatever the filter leaves. Deaths are
marked down the timeline spine on the left, so the shape of the run is visible
before you read a word of it.
